# Job Hunter AI - SaaS Product

## What It Does
AI-powered job application assistant that:
- Searches jobs across LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor
- Analyzes your match percentage with AI
- Generates custom CV for each specific job
- Writes personalized cover letters
- Tracks all your applications

## Pricing Tiers
- **Free**: 5 AI applications/month
- **Pro ($9.99/month)**: 50 applications/month
- **Premium ($29.99/month)**: Unlimited applications

## Tech Stack
- Backend: FastAPI (Python)
- Frontend: React + Vite
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Auth: Clerk
- Payments: Stripe
- Hosting: Railway + Vercel

## Revenue Goal
- Month 1: 10 users = $100/month
- Month 3: 100 users = $1,000/month
- Month 6: 500 users = $5,000/month
